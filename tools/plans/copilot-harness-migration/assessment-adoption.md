## Assessment adoption: proposed architecture, not runtime acceptance

The independent research-led assessment completed on 2026-10-18 recommends simplifying APEX rather than
preserving its existing framework wholesale. Its report, per-file disposition ledger and coverage evidence
remain in the owner's session-owned research storage outside the checkout.
This document makes the delivery decisions self-contained without publishing raw session/private project data.

### Independence and evidence limits

Repository guidance was already attached to the research session. No clean-room isolation or neutral-runtime
pilot was demonstrated. Discovery/tool contracts, official documentation, inspected source and historical
claims were distinguished. File inventory completeness did not mean every bundled reference was read.
Uninspected assets remain explicit evidence gaps; they are not approved for deletion.

### Outcomes to preserve

Live effective Azure governance; security baseline; approved SKU/region/tag decisions; AVM correctness;
cost provenance and deterministic calculation; compiler/schema checks; review independence and findings;
real approval separation; atomic/revision-safe durable writes; explicit recovery; historical compatibility.
Native SQL/todos do not replace durable cross-project business approvals.
Native role selection does not isolate shared Azure credentials.

### Mechanisms not presumed necessary

Numbered step-agent ownership, a forced orchestration DAG, handshake/nonce rituals, raw-log replay on each task,
duplicate review implementations, arbitrary line/read limits, vendor-specific XML skeletons and cosmetic H2 gates.
Keep the useful outcome before removing its existing mechanism. A passing prose assertion is not runtime proof.
An existing caller establishes migration cost, not architectural necessity.
